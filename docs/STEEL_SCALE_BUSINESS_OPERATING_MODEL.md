# Steel Scale Systems — sales-demo operating model

Approved September 7, 2026. This document describes the business process, not a claim that the software is live.

## Positioning

Steel Scale builds and operates business-specific automation for its own clients. Sell the operational result: faster response, better follow-up, fewer dropped inquiries, less manual coordination, and a clearer booking process. Use a personalized demonstration when it materially helps an interested prospect understand that result.

Do not add reseller or agency white-label offers. Keep Steel Scale as the operator. Preserve separate client data and systems.

## Four connected but independent systems

**Lead intelligence:** finds, enriches and prioritizes prospects. It never creates demos automatically.

**Outreach and sales workspace:** records cold calls, email/SMS interactions, qualification, suppression, follow-ups and sales calls. A prospect does not need a demo to move through sales.

**On-demand Demo Builder:** Carson intentionally enters a business or chooses an existing prospect, supplies relevant context, generates a draft, reviews it, publishes a link and decides whether to send it.

**Production fulfillment:** activates actual services only after a sale, approved onboarding and technical testing. A demo record is not a live Client.

## When to create a demo

Good trigger: a business owner expresses interest, describes a concrete response or follow-up problem, or agrees to a sales discussion where a tailored demonstration will clarify the solution.

Not a trigger: a new scraped row, a high lead score, an outbound message being sent, a call going unanswered or an automated status change. Carson can also prepare a standalone demo deliberately without importing the business into the lead database.

## What to enter

Business name and niche are the minimum. Add the website and GBP reference when useful, along with services, location, verified hours, contact reference and private call notes. Choose the modules that address the problem discussed. Mark unknown information as unknown; niche examples are not verified business facts.

For a prospect with slow Facebook lead follow-up, emphasize instant response, nurture, qualification and booking examples. For missed calls, emphasize recovery and receptionist workflows. For website work, show actual observations and a relevant chat experience. Do not claim the business needs more leads or has poor Google visibility unless evidence supports that.

## Quality gate before sharing

Verify the business identity, service descriptions, examples, public copy, enabled modules, limits and any financial assumptions. Keep internal comments, budget, contact details and sensitive sales notes off the public page. Confirm the link expiration and simulation labeling. Use the private preview when testing so your visits do not inflate engagement.

Share deliberately through an appropriate existing conversation; generating or opening a demo does not automatically enroll the prospect in outbound campaigns. Engagement does not override contact permissions or suppression.

## Sales follow-through

Treat opens and interactions as directional signals, not proof that the owner viewed the page or wants to buy. On the sales call, ask which workflow solves the problem they described, confirm actual volumes and economics, scope the implementation, and agree on a measurable outcome. Do not turn a hypothetical ROI scenario into a revenue guarantee.

## Handoff after a sale

Create/review onboarding separately. Confirm business settings, destinations, consent/communication preferences and integration authorization. Copy only approved business context from the demo. Never copy sample bookings, demo tokens, fake customer records or fictional financial defaults into production. Go live only after the real workflow has been tested.

## Initial implementation boundary

The accompanying package implements generation, review, sharing controls and guided examples. Real browser voice, LLM chat, advanced audit/GBP data, automatic engagement-to-queue integration and the client portal are still separate build milestones. The ongoing business rule is permanent: **demos are on demand, never a mandatory side effect of lead scraping.**
